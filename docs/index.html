How it works
1
Assign an AI prompt to a number key (1–0) in Slots.
2
Select text anywhere and press ⌘ ⌥ + the number — CopyCat transforms it with the prompt.
3
Paste the transformed text anywhere with ⌘ ⌥ ⇧ + the same number.
All AI processing happens locally on your device. Your text never leaves this machine — no cloud, no accounts, no telemetry.

Three steps. That's it.

Everything you need, nothing you don't.

CopyCat replaces your single clipboard with a powerful multi-slot system — with AI built in.

10 Clipboard Slots

Copy to any of 10 numbered slots (1–0) using keyboard shortcuts. Each slot holds your text independently.

Custom Shortcuts

Copy and paste from any slot instantly. Choose your own modifier keys — displayed with beautiful keycap-style visuals.

Local AI Transforms

Attach AI prompts to slots — summarize, translate, fix grammar, or any custom transformation. Powered by Ollama.

100% Private

Everything runs locally. No cloud, no accounts, no telemetry. Your clipboard data never leaves your machine.

Glassmorphic HUD

A beautiful floating overlay shows your filled slots at a glance. Customizable position, style, accent color, and duration.

Cross-Platform

Built with Rust and Tauri for native performance. Available on macOS (Apple Silicon & Intel) and Windows.
