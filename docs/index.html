Everything you need, nothing you don't.

CopyCat replaces your single clipboard with a powerful multi-slot system — with AI built in.

10 Clipboard Slots

Copy to any of 10 numbered slots (1–0) using keyboard shortcuts. Each slot holds your text independently.

Keyboard Shortcuts

Copy and paste from any slot instantly. ⌘⌥+1 to copy, ⌘⌥⇧+1 to paste. Fully customizable modifier keys.

Local AI Transforms

Attach AI prompts to slots — summarize, translate, fix grammar, or any custom transformation. Powered by Ollama.

100% Private

Everything runs locally. No cloud, no accounts, no telemetry. Your clipboard data never leaves your machine.

Glassmorphic HUD

A beautiful floating overlay shows your filled slots at a glance. Customizable position, style, accent color, and duration.

Cross-Platform

Built with Rust and Tauri for native performance. Available on macOS (Apple Silicon & Intel) and Windows.

Smart HUD

See your slots at a glance

The glassmorphic HUD floats above your workspace, showing only the slots that have content. It appears when you copy, then fades away — or keep it pinned with "Always visible" mode.

  • Warm glassmorphic, solid, or minimal style
  • 9 screen positions — pin it where you want
  • Custom accent color with live preview
  • Shows AI prompt labels on each slot
AI Prompts

Transform text as you copy

Create custom AI prompts and assign them to any slot. When you copy text to that slot, the local AI transforms it automatically — ready to paste.

  • Summarize articles into bullet points
  • Fix grammar and spelling instantly
  • Translate between languages
  • Create your own custom transformations
Full Control

Customize every shortcut

Set your own modifier keys for copy, paste, HUD toggle, and clear actions. The slot number (1–0) is added automatically — just pick the modifiers that feel right.

  • Configurable copy & paste modifier keys
  • Toggle HUD with a single shortcut
  • Clear all slots instantly
  • No conflicts with existing OS shortcuts

Three steps. That's it.

CopyCat works in the background — no windows to manage, no apps to switch to.

1

Copy to a slot

Select text and press your copy shortcut + a number.

⌘ + ⌥ + 1
2

AI transforms it

If the slot has a prompt, the local AI processes your text automatically.

✨ Local AI
3

Paste from the slot

Press your paste shortcut + the same number. Done.

⌘ + ⌥ + ⇧ + 1
